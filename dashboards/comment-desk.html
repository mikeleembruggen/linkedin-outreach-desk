How this works

Each morning a routine reads new comments on your recent LinkedIn posts and drafts a reply for each one. Approve a reply as written, or press Edit, change the words and press Save & approve.

Each afternoon a second routine posts every approved reply under its comment, exactly as shown here, then marks it Posted. Anything left in Needs review or Skipped is never posted. Undo moves an approved reply back to review any time before it goes out.
