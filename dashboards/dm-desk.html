How this works

Each morning a routine reads your LinkedIn inbox for new and unread messages you haven't answered, and drafts a reply for each conversation. Conversations it opens are marked unread again so your inbox still shows them.

Approve a reply as written, or press Edit, change the words and press Save & approve. Each afternoon a second routine sends every approved reply in its conversation, exactly as shown here, then marks it Sent. If you've already replied yourself, or the person has written again since the draft, it holds the reply back and tells you why.

Each card also says whether you've sent that person a voice note, checked against your connections tracker and the conversation itself. If you haven't, it suggests one with a talking point. Voice notes can only be recorded in the LinkedIn mobile app, so open their profile there, send it, then press I sent a voice note.

When a conversation is a good fit for a chat, the draft offers two open 30-minute times from your Cal.com Meeting calendar plus your booking link: a coffee or a Google Meet for people near Brisbane, a Google Meet for everyone else. When someone picks a time and gives their email, approving the card also books the meeting in your calendar before the reply goes out. Press Don't book to send the reply without booking.
